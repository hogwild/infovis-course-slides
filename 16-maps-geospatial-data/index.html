Mercator is conformal: angles and shapes are preserved locally, at infinitesimal scale, rather than keeping whole continents undistorted. Ask students to compare highlighted Greenland with Africa. Area inflation increases toward the poles, which this finite frame clips. Straight Mercator segments represent rhumb lines, so they support constant-compass-bearing navigation; they generally are not shortest great-circle routes. Albers preserves area ratios throughout the map. Ask students to locate Canada, the U.S., and the highlighted standard parallels at 30°N and 60°N.
